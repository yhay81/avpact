# AVPact adopters

This file records verified, opt-in adoption rather than treating stars or
downloads as proof of use.

No external adopters have been recorded yet.

If AVPact is part of a reproducible workflow, open a pull request adding an
entry with:

- a public project or organization name;
- the operation or workflow being used;
- the supported platform;
- the safety or verification decision AVPact improved;
- whether use repeated after at least 30 days;
- limitations, false refusals, or false confidence encountered;
- a link to public evidence when available;
- whether the entry may be referenced in release notes.

Private users may instead open a discussion describing the workflow without
identifying details. Aggregate claims will not be added here without the
user's permission.

Never submit confidential media, credentials, private paths, or an unreviewed
receipt.
